# The Case Being

*Rethinking investigations, operations, and the future direction of SOS Platform.*

This morning I woke up with an idea about the future direction of SOS Platform.

It started with the concept of an Investigation: a process in which an operator—or Author—can iteratively collect information, review the evidence, analyze the results, and decide on the next step. At the same time, the useful steps can gradually form a catalog of reusable building blocks for future investigations.

But one missing piece became increasingly obvious: what happens after the investigation?

An investigation can lead to a conclusion, but often that conclusion also requires an action. Something needs to be changed, restarted, corrected, or verified.

That initially made me think about creating a separate Operations module. The more I thought about it, however, the less practical that seemed. Investigative and operational work are often part of the same process. You collect evidence, perform an action, verify the result, collect more evidence, and continue until the problem is understood or resolved.

This led to a broader concept: the **Case**.

A Case can contain different catalog items. Some are investigative in nature, while others are operational. Together, they form one iterative process with Runs, results, analysis, outcomes, and eventually a conclusion.

It feels like a much stronger concept for SOS Platform, and it opens up considerably more possibilities than Investigation alone.

So naturally, I am now doing a refactor...
