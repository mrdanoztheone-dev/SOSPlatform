# Privacy

SOS Platform uses privacy-friendly audience measurement to understand which pages are visited and how visitors arrive at the website.

The website does not use advertising trackers.

Audience statistics are provided by GoatCounter. GoatCounter does not store IP addresses, use tracking cookies, store persistent identifiers in the browser, or share collected information with third parties.

For questions about this website or its privacy practices, please use the contact link on the homepage.
